# DexOS — Subscription Tiers (Free vs. Pro)

This document establishes the monetization design and tier value propositions for DexOS.

---

## 1. Always Free (Core Value Proposition)
*We never monetise behavioral change, core logging mechanics, or data privacy.*
- **Daily Habit Checklist (Zenith)**: Complete, skip, and edit habits.
- **Hydration & Sleep Input**: Standard logging views.
- **Runway Budget tracker**: Basic expense logging and Runway indicators.
- **Rank levels**: Progression milestones based on earned Zyrons.

---

## 2. Pro Plan (Premium Intelligence & Customization)
*Pro plan adds advanced telemetry analysis, custom design extensions, and momentum protection.*
- **AI Coach Synthesizer**: Unlocking Strict/Zen coach modes with automated daily telemetry summaries.
- **Streak Shield Protection**: Automated recovery prompts that protect streaks from resets (twice a month).
- **Historical Telemetry Charts**: Deeper Recharts visualization of trends (over 30 days).
- **Cosmetics Market**: Custom styling themes, badges, and avatar frames.

---

## 3. Team Plan (Accountability Echelon)
*Team tier focuses on collaborative habit networks and shared alerts.*
- **Accountability Circles**: Sync focus timers, group milestones, and budget runway boundaries between partners.
- **Sync Alerts**: Non-intrusive push notifications when a family budget Runway drops below 7 days.
